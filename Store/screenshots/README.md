# Phone screenshots

Put 2 to 8 portrait screenshots here (PNG or JPG). Play's rules: each side 320 to 3840 px, and the long side at most **twice** the short side.

- Your Galaxy A16 takes 1080 x 2340, which is a little too tall (2340 > 2 x 1080). Crop it to 1080 x 2160 or 1080 x 1920, or ask Claude to crop them.
- Take them over adb while the phone is connected:

      adb exec-out screencap -p > Store/screenshots/1-home.png

- Good picks: the home screen, a tangled level on a day sky, a hint pulsing, a night level, the "Bo Kata!" win screen.

The `draft-*.png` files are small previews from the desktop screenshot tour (540 x 923). They show which screens to take; replace them with real phone screenshots before uploading.
