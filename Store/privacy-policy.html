Kite Tangle - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Kite Tangle ("the app") is a free puzzle game published by ZekoClub ("we"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no account or sign-in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location. It contains no analytics software of its own. The only outside service it uses is Google AdMob for advertising, described below.

Information stored on your device

Your level, stars, coins, hints and settings are saved only on your own device so you can continue where you left off. They are not sent to us. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Kite Tangle shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (for an extra slot, another try or bonus coins), and now and then a short full-screen ad between levels. Google may use your device's advertising ID, general device information and how you interact with ads to show and measure them. In the European Economic Area and the UK, the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Virtual coins

Coins in the app are a virtual game currency with no real-world value. They cannot be bought with real money, cashed out, transferred or exchanged.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Permissions

The app uses the internet only to load ads. The game itself plays offline.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. We hold no other data about you.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what information it handles, this policy will be updated and the date at the top changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@zekoclub.com
