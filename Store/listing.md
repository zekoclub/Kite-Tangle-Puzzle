## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Basant is here and the sky is full of kites - and their strings are hopelessly tangled!

Every kite is tied to a charkhi on the rooftop, and the strings cross over each other. A kite can only be freed when no other string lies on top of its string. Tap the right kite and it flies to the chhat (rooftop) of its color. Fill a chhat with 3 kites and the next one arrives. Wrong color? The kite waits in a slot - but the slots fill up fast, so think before you tap!

HOW TO PLAY
- Tap a kite whose string lies ON TOP of every string it crosses
- It flies to the rooftop of its color, or waits in a free slot
- Fill every rooftop to win: Bo Kata!
- Tap a tangled kite and you lose a heart - 3 hearts per level

FEATURES
- 200+ hand-checked puzzles, every one solvable
- Easy to learn, with a short "How to play" for beginners
- Day, dusk and night skies with drifting clouds, moon and stars
- Earn up to 3 stars on every level
- Hints when you are stuck, and an extra slot when you need it
- Plays offline; no account needed
- Small download, runs smoothly on most phones

Inspired by the Basant kite festival: patang, dor, charkhi and chhat.
